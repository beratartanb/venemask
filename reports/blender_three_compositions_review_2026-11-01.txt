VENEMASK / THREE COMPOSITION STUDIES
Production and visual review report
Date: 2026-11-01

STATUS
The requested artistic standard has NOT been achieved or approved.
This is an unfinished three-still study, not a completed cinematic world.
The genuine La Regina Nera model remains unavailable; the main Hero product
composition is unfinished. Casanova is only a secondary provisional product.

SCOPE AND CHANGES MADE
A separate Blender scene was built from an empty scene for the Hero, Gallery
Reveal and Hall of Icons, using licensed source geometry and reusable import,
material and render helpers. The previous five-destination project is preserved.

Hero: revised canal staging and near-surface camera, dimensional architectural
facades, a carved wellhead supporting the display, and cloth geometry baked from
a collision simulation. The main product location remains empty.

Gallery: an enclosed vaulted composition with a recessed apse, gathered curtain,
carved display supports and a closer secondary Casanova composition.

Hall: an oblique staircase composition with a bronze Perseus sculpture, ribbed
vaults, lancet glazing, recessed architectural fields and carved foreground support.

These are actual geometry, material, camera and lighting changes. They do not
establish that the visual result is an improvement or meets the supplied references.

ACTUAL CURRENT RENDERS
All three were rendered in Blender 5.2 / Cycles on CPU at 1280x720, 40 samples.
- Hero: renders/final_hero.png; recorded render time 141.83 seconds.
- Gallery Reveal: renders/final_gallery.png; 167.35 seconds.
- Hall of Icons: renders/final_hall.png; 142.41 seconds.

The actual images were opened and visually inspected. The comparison board,
renders/reference_comparison.jpg, places the supplied references, rejected
baseline and new stills on equal 768x432 canvases with complete images letterboxed.
It is evidence for review, not evidence of artistic approval.

VISUAL FINDINGS
- Hero cloth still reads thin and flat despite the executed cloth simulation.
- The skyline reads as separate assemblies; the dark canal band weakens depth.
- Gallery surfaces remain broad and empty; distant practical fixtures are not
  convincingly grounded in the visible architecture.
- The Hall has carved foreground detail but still has plain wall masses and
  simplified architectural transitions.
- The requested painterly atmosphere, monumental sculptural unity, rich surface
  history and sophisticated chiaroscuro remain unmet.
More samples, resolution or animation frames would not resolve these design issues.

PRODUCT STATUS
La Regina Nera has not been imported because its genuine model was unavailable.
Its 0.8 x 0.4 x 0.8 metre placement envelope is an estimate, not measured product
dimensions. The current camera projects that estimate across approximately 35%
of the image width; this is not validation of a real product silhouette/contact.

Casanova uses authentic merchant OBJ geometry with reconstructed, provisional
materials. It appears in the Gallery as a secondary test, not as the feathered Hero.
Missing products are not represented by invented masks.
