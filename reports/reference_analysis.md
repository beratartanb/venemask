# Reference analysis — art direction only (not product truth)

Sources: `references/01-multi-mask-room-and-product-showcase.jpeg` (1536×1024),
`references/02-venetian-hero-wide-space.jpeg` (1536×864). Both are generated concept frames:
the masks in them are not Venemask products, and the $ prices, ratings and names
("Serenissima $189") are fictional and must not be used.

## 01 — multi-mask terrace / showcase
- **Camera:** ~35–40 mm equivalent, eye height slightly above the ledge, looking along a wet stone
  balustrade that recedes diagonally into the right-hand background. Strong one-point depth.
- **Planes:** foreground dark blurred cloth/stone (bottom-left), midground hero mask on the ledge
  (right-centre, ~35 % frame width incl. feathers), background canal + domed church silhouette (top-left),
  colonnade arches (top-centre).
- **Light:** warm 2200–2700 K practicals (candles, lanterns) at distance; warm key from the right on the
  mask; cool blue ambient from the night sky/water. Very low overall key — most of the frame is under
  10 % luminance.
- **Surfaces:** wet stone with mirror-like specular streaks; canal with long vertical reflections of
  every light; velvet cloth under the mask.
- **Composition:** dense — this is a UI concept with a collection strip and a best-sellers rail painted
  into the picture. For the physical scene the useful lessons are the *continuous wet ledge* that can
  carry several masks at different depths, and the *canal-side lights as bokeh*.

## 02 — Venetian hero, wide negative space
- **Camera:** ~50–65 mm equivalent, low (at the mask's eye line), mask on the right third in 3/4 profile
  facing frame-left; the left 45 % is dark negative space for the headline.
- **Depth:** mask sharp; second blurred mask mid-left; palazzo + dome across water soft but readable
  as shapes (≈ f/2.8–4 equivalent, not a black blur).
- **Light:** warm key from behind-right (candle) giving rim on feathers and the cheek; frontal fill is very
  low and slightly cool; moon as a small high-key accent; window lights as warm points.
- **Surfaces:** crackle-glaze face with gloss highlights, red velvet, rough stone.
- **What makes it read "expensive":** one dominant subject, extreme contrast ratio (~1:20), warm/cool
  separation, physically plausible bokeh, and almost no mid-tone clutter.

## Rules taken into the Unreal scene
1. One dominant real mask on a real stone surface; contact shadow visible.
2. Right-third subject, left negative space, horizon around the upper third.
3. Warm key + warm rim, cool sky fill; contrast ratio high; background only as defocused shapes/lights.
4. Wet stone + still canal to carry reflections — no added candles, statues, fog or glitter.
5. Camera lens 65 mm, f/2.0, focus on the mask's eyes (gives 02-style background softness).
